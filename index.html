Understanding Employee Attrition at RevoPraxio: An HR Analysis

Domain: Human Resources, Attrition Analytics

Tools: Google Spreadsheets, Python (Google Colab), Tableau

Objective: Reduce attrition rate from 16.12% (Caution Zone) to <10% within three months by identifying key drivers of voluntary turnover and providing data-driven retention strategies.

Approach:

  • Data cleaning & feature selection from IBM HR dataset (1470 employees, 35 features)
  • EDA on demographic, organizational, and relational factors
  • Logistic Regression for prediction and feature importance
  • Simulation for overtime reduction & relationship satisfaction

Key Insights:

  • Main drivers: Overtime, poor work-life balance, low income, job dissatisfaction, weak relationships, new leadership
  • Reducing overtime by 75% → attrition drops from 16.1% to ~9.0%
  • Improving relationships alone has limited impact

Business Recommendations:

  • Reduce overtime via workforce reallocation
  • Strengthen leadership alignment
  • Implement flexible schedule & wellness programs
  • Monitor predictors continuously via HR dashboards

Driving Credit Card Engagement: Sales Performance & Client Personas at RevoBank

Domain: Banking, Customer Segmentation & Credit Card Analysis

Tools: Python (Pandas, Numpy, Scikit-Learn), Google Colaboratory

Objective: To leverage transaction and customer data to increase credit card usage by analyzing performance, detecting risks, and identifying customer personas for targeted campaigns.

Approach:

  • Data cleaning & preparation (handling missing values, duplicates, expired/zero-limit cards).
  • Exploratory analysis of net profit, fraud rate, card brand performance, DTI, and credit limits.
  • K-Means clustering for customer segmentation (transaction + demographic + financial attributes).

Key Insights:

  • Profit: Rp 5.82B in 6 months, but fraud rate (0.22%) above global benchmark → risk zone.
  • Dormancy: ~33% of cards inactive (>180 days).
  • Customer Personas:

    Cluster 0: Dormant Young Customers → low limit, low activity.

    Cluster 1: Active Risky Spenders → high transactions, high debt-to-income.

    Cluster 2: Affluent Senior Customers → high spending, low risk.

Business Recommendations:

  • Reactivate dormant users via cashback and bonus points.
  • Apply stricter risk control for Active Risky Spenders.
  • Prioritize Affluent Seniors with premium benefits, loyalty programs, and cross-selling (insurance, investments).

Tools: SQL (Google BigQuery), Spreadsheets

  • Queried & analyzed sales database using SQL (BigQuery).

Propensity Model for PayLater Activation: RevoBank Pilot Program

Domain: Banking, Predictive Modelling

Tools: Python (Pandas, Numpy, Scikit-Learn), Google Colaboratory

Objective: To build a propensity model to predict which customers are most likely to adopt the new PayLater feature, enabling more effective targeting during the 3-month pilot program.

Approach:

  • Data cleaning & feature engineering (handling missing values, one-hot encoding, new activity flags).
  • Logistic Regression with class balancing for imbalanced dataset.
  • Model evaluation using ROC-AUC, lift, and decile analysis.

Key Insights:

  • Logistic Regression model achieved AUC = 0.83 → good discriminatory power.
  • Top 3 deciles (30% customers) captured >71% of adopters.
  • Model targeting provided 2.86× lift vs random targeting.
  • Key predictors: promo-related activity & account engagement, while age and gender had minimal impact.

Business Recommendations:

  • Use the model to prioritize high-probability customers, reducing campaign costs.
  • Focus marketing resources on top 30% deciles to achieve majority of adoption.
  • Integrate propensity modeling into ongoing campaigns to improve ROI.

Identifying Key Drivers of Customer Spending in TokoBli E-commerce

Domain: E-Commerce, Customer Spending Analysis

Tools: Statistics (XLMiner) and Google Spreadsheets

Objective: To identify and quantify the factors that significantly influence yearly customer spending to design strategies that increase revenue per customer.

Approach:

  • Data cleaning & preparation (removing irrelevant columns, ensuring valid types).
  • Correlation analysis to explore relationships among variables.
  • Backward stepwise regression to isolate key drivers of yearly spending.

Key Insights:

  • Income: higher-income customers tend to spend more overall.
  • Tenure: long-term customers consistently increase their spending.
  • Average Session Time: the strongest driver—longer browsing correlates with higher annual spending.
  • Other variables (Age, Bounce Rate, Promo usage) were not significant after regression testing.

Business Recommendations:

  • Target high-income customers with premium product campaigns.
  • Build structured loyalty programs (tiered rewards, milestones) to extend tenure.
  • Enhance app engagement with personalized recommendations, live shopping, and interactive content to increase session duration.

Analysis of TokoBli Twin Dates Campaign: Strategy & Results Evaluation

Domain: E-Commerce, Marketing Campaign Analysis

Tools: Statistics (XLMiner) and Google Spreadsheets

Objective: To evaluate the effectiveness of three “Twin Dates” campaigns (10.10, 11.11, 12.12) to provide recommendations for future campaign strategies.

Approach:

  • Data cleaning (handling missing values, correcting types, removing outliers).
  • Descriptive & exploratory data analysis.
  • Hypothesis testing (A/B testing on new product page design).

Key Insights:

  • Campaign 11.11 generated the highest revenue (Rp 8.24M) and was most successful overall.
  • Campaign 12.12 achieved the highest transactions but with lower revenue → lower product prices.
  • Discount efficiency varied: 12.12 was most efficient, 10.10 least.
  • Basket size remained low (1 item/transaction), highlighting potential for bundling & upselling.

Business Recommendations:

  • Implement bundling & cross-selling to increase average basket size.
  • Review discount efficiency and optimize allocation.
  • Retarget high-value customers with exclusive offers.
  • Pilot micro-campaigns in high-efficiency, low-volume categories (Books, School).

RevoFinance: Visualizing Personal Spending and Budget Insights

Domain: Fintech, Personal Finance Visualization

Tools: Tableau

Objective: To design an interactive dashboard to help users track personal expenses, monitor budget utilization, and identify spending patterns for better financial planning.

Approach:

  • Integrated three datasets: Personal Expenses, Merchant Master, and Annual Budget.
  • Created table relationships in Tableau (logical layer) to maintain level of detail.
  • Built dashboards with metrics: total expense, average transaction, spending distribution, budget vs actual.

Key Insights:

  • Total annual spending reached Rp508M, with budget utilization at 93.7% (under budget).
  • Top 3 categories: Utilities, Dining, and Transport; Education had the lowest share.
  • Overspending occurred in Education, Entertainment, Subscription, and Travel.
  • Spending peaked in December (Rp54M), lowest in October (Rp35M), showing strong year-end seasonality.
  • Offline spending concentrated in Jakarta, especially on utilities, groceries, and dining.

Business Recommendations:

  • Reallocate underutilized budgets to categories with frequent overspending.
  • Promote financial literacy by encouraging more allocation toward productive categories like Education.
  • Plan budget buffers for year-end when spending spikes.
